About

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from BRAC University with a strong interest in exploring how technology works across different layers of computing. I enjoy learning by building, experimenting with ideas, and solving practical problems through software and hardware.

My projects span machine learning and deep learning, including medical image classification and segmentation; computer vision and data science; systems programming, including operating system scheduling and filesystem journaling; embedded systems and IoT; full-stack web development and database-driven applications; 3D graphics and interactive simulations; and cloud security and AI-assisted access control research.

I enjoy working at the intersection of software, data, hardware, and security—whether that means developing an intelligent system, implementing a low-level computing concept, building a connected device, or designing an application that solves a real-world problem. I'm continuously exploring new technologies and strengthening my ability to turn ideas into working systems.
