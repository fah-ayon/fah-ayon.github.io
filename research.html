Enhancing Cloud Security for Healthcare Data: AI-Driven Encryption and Access Optimization

Problem

Healthcare systems need to protect sensitive records while supporting practical access for staff and patients in cloud environments.

Approach

The prototype assigns sensitivity labels and combines ABAC and BBAC policy data with Random Forest and Isolation Forest models for AI-driven staff access decisions. Patients use direct private-key access.

Exposure intelligence

Access behaviour is scored to generate exposure advisories and recommend key rotation or monitoring intensity without changing the encryption level selected at provisioning.

Encryption and storage

Records use fixed multi-level encryption, wrapped key management, cloud object storage, and an L3 homomorphic encryption demonstration for highly sensitive data.
