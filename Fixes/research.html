Securing Healthcare Data in the Cloud: AI-Assisted Adaptive Access Control and Tiered Encryption with Post-Access Advisory Exposure Engine

Abstract

Migrating Electronic Health Records (EHRs) to the cloud delivers scalability and accessibility, but it also exposes highly sensitive medical data to external breaches and insider threats, while conventional defenses rely on heavy cryptographic mechanisms whose computational latency conflicts with the real-time demands of clinical care. This research presents a zero-trust, AI-assisted framework that combines adaptive access control with sensitivity-tiered encryption to secure cloud-hosted health-care data without compromising clinical efficiency. The system follows a decoupled three-stage design: first, records are classified into a four-tier sensitivity hierarchy (L0–L3), where standard records (L0–L2) are protected with AES-256-GCM and ECC-based key wrapping, while the most sensitive data, such as genomic vectors (L3), use CKKS homomorphic encryption to permit privacy-preserving computation in the cloud, with only ciphertext ever pushed to Google Cloud Storage so that cryptographic keys never cross the cloud boundary; second, a hybrid machine-learning gatekeeper (Random Forest and Isolation Forest) evaluates contextual staff behavior to enforce real-time permit, step-up (MFA), or deny decisions before any retrieval; and finally, an advisory Exposure Intelligence engine analyzes append-only local audit logs to score behavior longitudinally and recommend targeted key-rotation policies. Evaluated on a fully synthetic, HIPAA and FHIR-aligned dataset and a 4,000-record encrypted cohort, the framework achieves strong, standards-based privacy preservation entirely on commodity CPU hardware, with the complete audited decryption path executing in approximately 516 ms, well within interactive clinical tolerances. By avoiding GPU-dependent infrastructure, this work offers a verifiable and resource-efficient blueprint for accessible, robust medical cloud security.

Research focus

A zero-trust, AI-assisted framework for securing cloud-hosted electronic health records, combining sensitivity-tiered encryption, adaptive access control, and post-access exposure analysis.

Methods and technologies

Four data-sensitivity tiers (L0–L3) AES-256-GCM ECC key wrapping (L0–L2) CKKS homomorphic encryption (L3) Random Forest Isolation Forest Permit / step-up (MFA) / deny Append-only audit logs Google Cloud Storage

Key contribution

Combines adaptive pre-access authorization, tiered encryption, and longitudinal post-access advisory analysis in a decoupled three-stage design. The approach is resource-efficient and avoids GPU-dependent infrastructure.

The evaluation uses a fully synthetic dataset. Results are experimental and have not been validated in a clinical setting. This work is unpublished.
