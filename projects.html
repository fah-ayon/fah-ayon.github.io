Medical imagingTeam

NeuroSeg: Brain Tumor Classification and Segmentation

A deep-learning framework that classifies tumor types and segments tumor regions in MRI scans. Compares separate and joint multi-task training, a U-Net classifier/encoder, standard U-Net, and Attention U-Net on the BRISC 2025 dataset (6,000 MRI samples).

PyTorchU-NetAlbumentationsOpenCV

Result: 98.60% classification accuracy, 77.34% segmentation mIoU, 86.62% Dice, and 30% faster training with joint learning. Experimental results, not clinical validation.

Deep learningSolo

Deep CNN Classifier for CIFAR-10

A VGG-inspired CNN with 13 convolutional layers, batch normalization, dropout, data augmentation, AdamW, learning-rate scheduling, and early stopping. Includes training, evaluation, and visualization workflows.

PyTorchtorchvisionscikit-learn

Result: 93.14% final test accuracy (9,314 of 10,000 images). Training took 27.8 minutes for 200 epochs on an NVIDIA RTX 5070.

Classical MLSolo

Disease Classification Using Machine Learning

An exploratory study of disease-category classification from health indicators and lifestyle features. Uses imputation, label encoding, scaling, K-Means, PCA, and three classifiers (MLP, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression).

PythonScikit-learnPandasSeaborn

Result: About 1,800 records, 70/30 split. Best result was roughly 35% accuracy (Decision Tree, AUC 0.50). Feature correlations were weak, so this is not a diagnostic system.
